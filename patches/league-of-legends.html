League of Legends patch notes: Patch 26.23: Pro-prison buffs, ARAM Mayhem & Arena tweaks

Source: League of Legends Patch 26.23 Notes · Published 2026-11-17 · Summarized by the Prompt Medic Discord bot

TL;DR: top changes

  • Buffs to Annie, Azir, Bard, Ezreal, Galio, Gnar, K'Sante, Mel, Vi
  • Nerfs to LeBlanc, Master Yi, Syndra Q ratios and cooldowns
  • Voltaic Cyclosword trigger damage reduced
  • ARAM Mayhem: New headliners, tank augments, turret plating added
  • Arena balance changes to multiple champions and items

⬆️ Buffs

  • Annie: Tibbers health, aura damage and AD increased with new HP ratio
  • Azir: Attack range up to 550, W on-hit ratio to 67%
  • Bard: Q damage and AP ratio increased
  • Ezreal: W and R bonus AD/AP ratios buffed
  • Galio: W shield % max HP increased
  • Gnar: Q AD ratios increased for mini and mega forms
  • K'Sante: W mana cost flat, base damage up, charge scaling adjusted
  • Mel: Q damage and ratios up, W shield and retaliation buffed

⬇️ Nerfs

  • LeBlanc: Q AP ratio reduced to 35%
  • Master Yi: Q AD ratio and max single target damage reduced
  • Syndra: E mana cost to 60, cooldown increased early
  • Voltaic Cyclosword: Firmament bonus damage lowered to 8%/6%

Meta impact

Buffs target pro staples like Azir, Ezreal and Gnar to improve solo queue viability while curbing LeBlanc and Master Yi dominance. ARAM Mayhem gains tank tools and turret defenses to slow games. Arena shifts favor alternative builds like AP Bel'Veth and Tristana.
