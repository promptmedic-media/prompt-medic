League of Legends patch notes: LoL 26.22: Corki/Rumble buffs, Locke/Yasuo nerfs, Mel midscope

Source: League of Legends Patch 26.22 Notes · Published 2026-11-03 · Summarized by the Prompt Medic Discord bot

TL;DR: top changes

  • Corki and Rumble R buffs for pro play
  • Locke and Yasuo nerfs to early power
  • Mel midscope moves power to scaling
  • Doran's Bow nerfed for ADC builds
  • ARAM augment balance and map returns

⬆️ Buffs

  • Aurelion Sol: Singularity damage and AP ratio increased
  • Corki: Missile Barrage and Big One damage up
  • Kayle: Q and R cooldowns reduced
  • Maokai: Bramble Smash %HP damage increased
  • Naafiri: Q heal and W AD increased
  • Rumble: Equalizer AP ratio improved
  • Shen: Q monster damage modifier up
  • Smolder: W explosion damage increased

⬇️ Nerfs

  • Locke: Q stack damage and W duration reduced
  • Thresh: Base AD and R damage lowered
  • Yasuo: Q total AD ratio reduced
  • Doran's Bow: Attack Damage reduced to 7

Meta impact

Corki and Rumble gain viability outside pro play while Locke and Yasuo see reduced early dominance. Mel's changes shift her toward late-game scaling with reworked W. Doran's Bow nerf encourages more varied ADC itemization on Rift.
