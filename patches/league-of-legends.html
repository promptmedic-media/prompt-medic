League of Legends patch notes: Worlds patch: Ambessa/K'Sante nerfs, Rocketbelt changes

Source: League of Legends Patch 26.20 Notes · Published 2026-10-06 · Summarized by the Prompt Medic Discord bot

TL;DR: top changes

  • Ambessa Passive damage reduced
  • K'Sante W mana cost up, damage reduction down
  • Hextech Rocketbelt AH reduced, active damage increased
  • Kennen E and Kindred W/E buffs
  • Multiple Classic mode champions added

⬆️ Buffs

  • Diana: W shield bonus health ratio 11% → 14%
  • Kennen: E bonus AS 40/50/60/70/80% → 50/60/70/80/90%
  • Kindred: W monster mod 150% → 165%, E slow 1s → 1.5s
  • Lillia: R duration now scales 2.5/2.75/3s
  • Lucian: AD per level 2.5 → 2.9
  • Mordekaiser: Q scaling +7/lvl, R CD reduced
  • Neeko: E base damage and root duration increased
  • Smolder: Passive crit scaling and R heal increased

⬇️ Nerfs

  • Ambessa: Passive damage 5-30 (+25% bAD) → 5-25 (+20% bAD)
  • Ashe: AD per level 3.5 → 3
  • Cassiopeia: Base HP 630 → 610, base mana 480 → 450
  • K'Sante: W mana cost increased, W DR 30/75% → 25/70%
  • Yunara: Q on-hit damage reduced, W slow no longer stacks
  • Experimental Hexplate: Ranged mod reduced to 50%
  • Runaan's Hurricane: AD per bolt 65% → 60%
  • Graves: Base AD 54.1 → 50, AD/level 3.1 → 2.5

Meta impact

Nerfs to pro staples Ambessa and K'Sante plus Rocketbelt changes should reduce their dominance heading into Worlds. Buffs to Kennen, Kindred, and others aim to bring more variety into viability. Classic mode additions and Mayhem tweaks add side content without shifting Rift meta.

Breakdown

  • Ambessa: Passive damage reduced for late game reliability
  • Ashe: AD per level reduced to lower raw damage
  • Cassiopeia: Base HP and mana reduced for early laning
  • Diana: W shield health ratio increased for bruiser builds
  • Kennen: E bonus attack speed increased
  • Kindred: W monster damage and E slow duration increased
  • K'Sante: W mana cost up, damage reduction reduced
  • Lillia: R duration now scales with rank
  • Lucian: AD per level increased
  • Mordekaiser: Q late scaling and R cooldown improved
  • Neeko: E damage and empowered root duration increased
  • Smolder: Passive crit scaling and R self-heal increased
  • Hextech Rocketbelt: AH reduced, active damage and AP ratio increased
  • Experimental Hexplate: Ranged effectiveness reduced
  • Runaan's Hurricane: AD per bolt reduced
