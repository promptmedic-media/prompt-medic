League of Legends patch notes: Patch 26.21: Caitlyn/Jinx/Lee nerfs, Varus fix, item tweaks

Source: League of Legends Patch 26.21 Notes · Published 2026-10-20 · Summarized by the Prompt Medic Discord bot

TL;DR: top changes

  • Caitlyn and Jinx nerfed for high presence
  • Lee Sin and Rammus power reduced
  • Varus Q bug fixed with compensation buffs
  • Gluttonous Greaves and Hubris nerfed for ranged
  • Buffs to Azir, Orianna, Ryze, Sion, Nunu

⬆️ Buffs

  • Azir: Mana Growth 40 to 50 per level
  • Darius: Q cooldown reduced at rank 5
  • Miss Fortune: E and R damage increased
  • Nunu & Willump: R max damage increased
  • Orianna: Q/W AP ratios increased
  • Ryze: Q flux and MS improved, W AP ratio up
  • Sion: Health growth increased, R channel damage up
  • Taliyah: E damage increased

⬇️ Nerfs

  • Caitlyn: Passive headshot damage down, Q base adjusted
  • Jinx: AD growth and base health reduced
  • Lee Sin: W cooldown to 8s, R damage reduced
  • Nidalee: W damage and E AS reduced
  • Qiyana: Q damage reduced
  • Rammus: W armor/MR bonuses reduced
  • Tristana: Q early attack speed reduced
  • Viktor: MS to 330, Q shield reduced

Meta impact

Nerfs to dominant marksmen and junglers like Caitlyn, Jinx, Lee Sin, and Rammus should open up the meta for other picks. Mages like Azir, Orianna, and Ryze gain breathing room while item changes curb ranged abuse of Greaves and Hubris. Expect shifts toward more varied bot and jungle compositions.
