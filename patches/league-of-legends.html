League of Legends patch notes: 26.21: Caitlyn/Jinx nerfs, Azir buffs, ARAM Mayhem

Source: League of Legends Patch 26.21 Notes · Published 2026-10-20 · Summarized by the Prompt Medic Discord bot

TL;DR: top changes

  • Caitlyn passive/Q adjusted, Jinx base stats nerfed
  • Lee Sin W/R and Nidalee W/E nerfed
  • Azir mana growth, Ryze Q/W, Sion base/R buffed
  • Hubris/Greaves nerfed for ranged, Dawncore AP buffed
  • New ARAM Mayhem 'Journey to Stardom' with augments

⬆️ Buffs

  • Azir: Mana Growth 40 to 50 per level
  • Darius: Q cooldown reduced at rank 5
  • Miss Fortune: E and R damage increased
  • Nunu: R max damage increased
  • Orianna: Q/W AP ratios increased
  • Ryze: Q flux damage and MS improved
  • Sion: Health growth and R damage increased
  • Taliyah: E damage increased

⬇️ Nerfs

  • Caitlyn: Passive headshot damage reduced
  • Jinx: AD and health growth reduced
  • Lee Sin: W cooldown increased, R damage reduced
  • Nidalee: W damage and E AS reduced
  • Qiyana: Q damage reduced
  • Rammus: W bonus armor/MR reduced
  • Tristana: Q early AS reduced
  • Yone: Q AD ratio reduced

Meta impact

Nerfs to dominant marksmen Caitlyn/Jinx and junglers Lee Sin/Nidalee/Rammus reduce their presence and open picks for others. Buffs to Azir, Orianna, Ryze, and Sion plus item tweaks encourage mid diversity and shift builds away from ranged Hubris/Greaves abuse. ARAM Mayhem adds new augments and progression for fresh gameplay.

Breakdown

  • Azir: Mana Growth increased to 50/level
  • Caitlyn: Passive headshot and Q damage adjusted
  • Jinx: AD and health growth reduced
  • Lee Sin: W cooldown up, R damage down
  • Nidalee: W damage and E AS reduced
  • Orianna: Q/W AP ratios increased
  • Ryze: Q flux/MS and W AP ratio improved
  • Sion: Health growth and R damage buffed
  • Hubris: Ranged AD and stacks reduced
  • Gluttonous Greaves: Cost increased to 1100g
  • Dawncore: AP per 100% base MR increased to 15
  • Varus: Q bugfix and passive buff applied
  • Rammus: W bonus armor/MR reduced
  • Tristana: Q early AS reduced
  • Yone: Q AD ratio reduced to 105%
