Valorant patch notes: VALORANT 13.08: Map Rating, Corrode Updates & Map Pool Swap

Source: VALORANT Patch Notes 13.08 · Published 2026-10-13 · Summarized by the Prompt Medic Discord bot

TL;DR: top changes

  • Map Rating system added for Competitive/Premier
  • Corrode map changes to defender paths, mid, and plant
  • Map pool: Bind & Corrode in, Ascent & Split out
  • Performance Score adjustments for damage/kills/deaths
  • Vanguard ban numbers shared for Act 5

⬆️ Buffs

None in this patch.

⬇️ Nerfs

None in this patch.

Meta impact

Map rotation forces adaptation to Bind and Corrode playstyles. Map Rating pushes players to specialize on maps for better grades. Performance Score tweaks better value utility and first bloods across agents.

Breakdown

  • Killjoy: Alarmbot VFX/audio now triggers on moving doorway destruction
  • Map Rating: New 0-500 rating with C-S grades based on win rates and Performance Score
  • Performance Score: Increased impact of Damage, Adjusted Kills, Adjusted Deaths; Chamber utility tweaks
  • Corrode - Defender Paths: Adjusted A/B site paths to shorten rotations for retakes
  • Corrode - Mid: Half wall moved 1m, metal material; boxes relocated, wall raised
  • Corrode - A Site: Plant zone adjusted to reduce A Link post-plant effectiveness
  • Competitive Queue: Bind and Corrode added; Ascent and Split removed
  • Client: New Queue/Mode Select interface and Career navigation bar
