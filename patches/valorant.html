Valorant patch notes: VALORANT 13.10: Replays Bookmarks, Sentinel Tweaks & Gauntlet Glitched

Source: VALORANT Patch Notes 13.10 · Published 2026-11-10 · Summarized by the Prompt Medic Discord bot

TL;DR: top changes

  • Replay bookmarking added for personal use
  • Sentinel destroy cooldowns reduced to 45s
  • Assist rules updated for damage and healing
  • Gauntlet: Glitched adds new abilities, glitches, and balance
  • Sage Healing Orb targeting improved

⬆️ Buffs

  • Cypher Spycam: Destroy cooldown reduced 60s to 45s
  • Killjoy Turret: Destroy cooldown reduced 60s to 45s
  • Vyse Arc Rose: Destroy cooldown reduced 60s to 45s
  • Sage Healing Orb: Increased ally targeting accuracy
  • Gauntlet Hush Flow: Additional charges on Prone to Flight

⬇️ Nerfs

  • Cypher Spycam: Reclaim cooldown increased 15s to 20s
  • Blaze Quickburn: Recharge cooldowns increased at levels 2-3
  • Boom Bot Overclocked: Movement speed and duration reduced
  • Contingency Redundancy: Added recharge cooldowns and fewer charges

Meta impact

Sentinels gain faster resets after utility destruction, promoting earlier setups and mid-round recovery. Updated assists better reward trading and healing impact. Gauntlet mode expands with new abilities and glitches, shifting draft priorities toward versatile upgrades.

Breakdown

  • Cypher: Spycam destroy cooldown 60s to 45s, reclaim 15s to 20s
  • Killjoy: Turret destroy cooldown 60s to 45s
  • Vyse: Arc Rose destroy cooldown 60s to 45s
  • Sage: Healing Orb ally targeting accuracy increased, crosshair visible
  • Damage Assists: Now awarded for >10 damage within 3s of kill or 50+ total
  • Healing Assists: Only if healed player kills within 10s after heal ends
  • Gauntlet Hush: New abilities: Flow, Displacement, Suspension ultimate
  • Gauntlet Glitches: Cypher Spycam and Skye Trailblazer get gun variants
