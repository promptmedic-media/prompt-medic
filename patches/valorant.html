Valorant patch notes: Patch 13.10: Replay bookmarks, Sentinel cooldowns, Gauntlet updates

Source: VALORANT Patch Notes 13.10 · Published 2026-11-10 · Summarized by the Prompt Medic Discord bot

TL;DR: top changes

  • Added personal replay bookmarking feature
  • Reduced Cypher/Killjoy/Vyse destroy cooldowns to 45s
  • Updated damage and healing assist rules
  • Gauntlet: Glitched adds new abilities, glitches, and balance tweaks
  • Multiple agent and map bug fixes

⬆️ Buffs

  • Cypher Spycam: destroy cooldown 60s to 45s
  • Killjoy Turret: destroy cooldown 60s to 45s
  • Vyse Arc Rose: destroy cooldown 60s to 45s
  • Sage Healing Orb: improved ally targeting accuracy
  • Gauntlet Hush Flow: additional charges
  • Gauntlet Prowler Chonky Cat: health increased

⬇️ Nerfs

  • Cypher Spycam: reclaim cooldown 15s to 20s
  • Gauntlet Blaze Quickburn: recharge cooldowns increased
  • Gauntlet Boom Bot Overclocked: speed and duration reduced
  • Gauntlet Contingency Redundancy: added recharge, fewer charges
  • Gauntlet Headhunter: added recharge, less ammo

Meta impact

Sentinel cooldown reductions encourage more utility placement and resets while rewarding destruction. Assist changes promote trading and reward impactful healing. Gauntlet balance aims to diversify viable upgrades in the mode.

Breakdown

  • Replay Bookmarking: Personal bookmarks for key moments in replays
  • Cypher Spycam: Destroy cooldown reduced to 45s, reclaim to 20s
  • Killjoy Turret: Destroy cooldown reduced to 45s
  • Vyse Arc Rose: Destroy cooldown reduced to 45s
  • Sage Healing Orb: Better targeting accuracy and visible crosshair
  • Damage Assists: Now awarded for >10 damage in 3s window
